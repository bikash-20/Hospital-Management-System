# Product Requirement Document (PRD)

**Project Name:** CareBridge RMS (Resource & Patient Workflow Management System)
**Target Environment:** Clinics & Regional Hospitals (e.g., Popular / Ibn Sina Sylhet Model)  
**Deployment Cost Target:** $0 / Month (Fully Free Tier Architecture)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

CareBridge RMS is an end-to-end operational software designed to unify patient registration, doctor consultations, lab requests, pharmacy inventory, and billing into a high-efficiency single workflow. The primary goal is to minimize patient wait times, prevent duplicate record creation, eliminate paper-based miscommunications, and provide hospital management with real-time operational visibility.

---

## 2. Target Audience & User Roles

* **Patient:** Token tracking, live queue monitoring, viewing prescriptions & reports.
* **Receptionist:** Patient registration, duplicate search, OPD slot booking, billing generation.
* **Doctor:** Electronic Health Record (EHR) entry, prescription writing, lab/radiology ordering, medical history review.
* **Lab Technician:** Processing test requests, uploading report PDFs/values, releasing results.
* **Pharmacist:** Inventory management, order fulfillment based on doctor prescriptions.
* **Cashier/Billing Officer:** Invoice settlement, payment collection, discount approvals.
* **Admin/Superuser:** Audit logs, revenue reports, bed management, role permission controls.
